Donate with Monero (XMR)

Secure peer-to-peer donations.

  1. Open your crypto wallet app on your phone
  2. Make sure you have some cryptocurrency available
  3. Point your phone camera at this QR code
  4. Or manually scan with your wallet app's QR scanner
  5. The wallet will recognize the address and pre-fill the details
  6. Review the wallet address carefully before proceeding
  7. Enter the amount you wish to donate
  8. Check the transaction fee (usually minimal for crypto)
  9. Confirm the transaction in your wallet
  10. Your donation is sent instantly to our wallet
